About

I build data-driven solutions for spatial problems, from satellite image processing to machine learning and web maps.

I am a remote sensing and GIS student at COMSATS University with a solid background in image processing, geospatial analysis and Python programming. I use Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS and QGIS, and I am comfortable moving a project between all three.

My work so far covers vegetation change, flood mapping, urban growth prediction and workflow automation. At the Pakistan Meteorological Department I gained practical exposure to glaciers, glacial lakes and weather patterns, and learned how to connect several GIS tools into one workflow.

Outside of GIS I design and build websites for local businesses, and I have a background in video and audio production for news. I learn quickly and I value teamwork.
