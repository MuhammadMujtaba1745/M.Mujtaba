About

I am a remote sensing and GIS student who builds clear, well-documented spatial analysis from satellite imagery.

I study BS Remote Sensing and GIS at COMSATS University and I am in my 7th semester. My projects use satellite imagery from several sensors and cover glacier retreat, groundwater, vegetation, burn severity and land surface temperature. I use supervised and unsupervised classification, train machine learning models for prediction, and write custom QGIS plugins to automate repeated work.

Through internships at Survey of Pakistan and the Pakistan Meteorological Department I gained practical experience in photogrammetry, UAV data collection, and the study of glaciers and weather patterns.

Objective

To grow as a remote sensing and GIS analyst by applying satellite imagery, machine learning and automation to environmental monitoring and water-resource questions, and to contribute carefully documented work to a research or applied geospatial team.

Case study

Gilgit Glacier Retreat

Study area
Gilgit, Pakistan
Period
2015 to 2025
Data
Sentinel-2

Objective

To map how glacier extent in the Gilgit region changed between 2015 and 2025 and to visualise the pattern of glacier retreat.

Workflow

  1. Collect Sentinel-2 imagery covering the Gilgit area for the 2015 to 2025 period.
  2. Prepare the imagery for analysis.
  3. Delineate glacier extent across the study period.
  4. Compare extents over time to identify where the glaciers have retreated.
  5. Produce the final map and check the findings against published research.

Validation

Results were checked against findings from published research on glacier change in the region.

Output

A map of glacier extent change in the Gilgit region from 2015 to 2025.

Case study

Islamabad Underground Water Analysis

Study area
Islamabad, Pakistan
Period
2025
Data
Sentinel-2

Objective

To assess groundwater-related conditions across Islamabad in 2025 and present them as a clear spatial map that supports understanding of local water resources.

Workflow

  1. Collect Sentinel-2 imagery and related spatial layers for Islamabad.
  2. Prepare and process the layers for analysis.
  3. Combine the layers in a GIS to assess groundwater-relevant conditions across the city.
  4. Produce the final map and check the findings against published studies.

Validation

Results were checked against findings from published research on groundwater in the area.

Output

A map of groundwater-related conditions across Islamabad for 2025.

Case study

Land Surface Temperature, Pakistan

Study area
Pakistan

Objective

To map land surface temperature across Pakistan and show how surface temperature varies between regions.

Workflow

  1. Collect satellite thermal data covering Pakistan.
  2. Process the imagery to derive land surface temperature.
  3. Map land surface temperature across the country.
  4. Interpret regional variation and check the findings against published research.

Validation

Results were checked against findings from published research.

Output

A land surface temperature map of Pakistan.
