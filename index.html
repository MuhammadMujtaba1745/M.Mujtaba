Gilgit Glacier Retreat, 2015 to 2025

Objective

To measure how glaciers around Gilgit changed over a ten-year period and to show where retreat is most pronounced.

Study area and dates

Gilgit, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. 2015 to 2025.

Data source

Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery.

Workflow and tools

  1. Select cloud-free Sentinel-2 scenes for the start and end of the study period.
  2. Delineate glacier extent in each period.
  3. Compare the two extents to quantify retreat.
  4. Produce change maps for presentation.

Tools: Google Earth Engine, QGIS.

Validation and limitations

Results were validated against published research on glacier change in the region. No field measurements were used. Snow cover, shadow and cloud can affect glacier boundaries in optical imagery.

Islamabad Underground Water Analysis, 2025

Objective

To assess groundwater conditions across Islamabad using satellite data and GIS analysis, and to identify areas of higher and lower groundwater availability.

Study area and dates

Islamabad Capital Territory, Pakistan. 2025.

Data source

Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery.

Workflow and tools

  1. Prepare Sentinel-2 imagery for the study area.
  2. Derive the spatial layers used in the assessment.
  3. Combine the layers in GIS to map groundwater conditions.
  4. Present the result as classified maps.

Tools: Google Earth Engine, QGIS, ArcGIS.

Validation and limitations

Results were validated against published studies on groundwater in Islamabad. No field well data was used, so the map shows relative conditions rather than measured water levels.

LST Pakistan

Objective

To map how land surface temperature varies across Pakistan and to show which regions are the hottest and coolest.

Workflow and tools

  1. Prepare satellite imagery for the whole country.
  2. Calculate land surface temperature.
  3. Classify and map temperature zones.

Tools: Google Earth Engine, QGIS.

Validation and limitations

Results were compared with temperature patterns reported in published research. Land surface temperature differs from air temperature, and cloud cover limits usable observations.
